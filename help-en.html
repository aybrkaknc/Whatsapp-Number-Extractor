WA Extractor - How to Use

Getting Started

1
Open WhatsApp Web in your browser and log in to your account.
2
Navigate to the group chat you want to extract members from.
3
Click on the group name at the top to open the group info panel, then click "Search participants" to open the members modal.

Extracting Numbers

4
Click the WA button in the bottom-right corner of the screen to open the extension panel.
5
Press the "Start" button. The extension will begin scanning visible members.
6
For large groups, use "Auto Scroll" to automatically scroll through the entire member list.

Saving Your Data

7
Use "Copy to Clipboard" to copy all collected numbers to your clipboard, or press "Finish & DL" to download a CSV file.

Extra Features

A
Use the TR/EN button in the title bar to switch between Turkish and English instantly.
B
You can drag the extension window anywhere on the screen by grabbing the title bar.
C
Click "System Logs" to monitor the extraction process in real time.
Note: This tool is designed for legitimate business and personal networking purposes. Please respect privacy and WhatsApp's terms of service.
