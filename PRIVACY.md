# Privacy Policy

[Türkçe](PRIVACY.tr.md)

WhatsApp Number Extractor runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com`.

- It reads the member list shown in the "Search participants" modal only after you press Start.
- Collected names and phone numbers stay in the page's memory until you download them, copy them, or press Finish & DL.
- No data is sent to any server, stored by the extension, or shared with third parties.
- The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics.

You are responsible for using exported data in line with applicable privacy laws and WhatsApp's terms of service.
