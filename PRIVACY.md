# Privacy Policy

[Türkçe](PRIVACY.tr.md)

WhatsApp Number Extractor runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com`.

- It reads the member list shown in the "Search participants" modal only after you press Start.
- Collected names and phone numbers stay in the page's memory until you download them, copy them, or press Finish & DL.
- Collected names and numbers are never sent to any server, stored by the extension, or shared with third parties.
- Only your settings (language, default country, CSV delimiter, scroll speed, copy format, sound) are saved, in the browser's extension storage on your device. This is why the extension asks for the "storage" permission.
- If you turn on "Keep the list on page reload", the collected list is also kept in this tab's session storage on web.whatsapp.com until you press Finish & DL or close the tab. It is off by default.
- The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics.

You are responsible for using exported data in line with applicable privacy laws and WhatsApp's terms of service.
