# Privacy Policy

[Türkçe](PRIVACY.tr.md)

Last updated: 9 November 2026

WhatsApp Number Extractor ("the extension") is a browser extension for Firefox and Chrome. This policy explains what the extension handles and what it does not.

## Where the extension runs

The extension runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com`. It does nothing on any other website.

## What the extension reads

- Only after you press **Start**, it reads the names and phone numbers of the group members shown in WhatsApp Web's "Search participants" list. It also reads the group name from the open chat header and the admin label of a member, when shown.
- It does not read your chats, messages, calls, contacts list or any other part of WhatsApp.

## Where the data is kept

- **Collected names and phone numbers** are kept only in the memory of the open WhatsApp Web tab, until you download, copy them, or close the tab.
- **Optional, off by default:** if you turn on "Keep the list on page reload", the list is also kept in the tab's session storage on `web.whatsapp.com`. It is removed when you press **Finish & DL**, when you turn the option off, or when you close the tab.
- **Settings** (interface language, default country for local numbers, CSV delimiter, auto scroll speed, copy format, export format, export country filter, "only contacts not saved in my phone" filter, sound, animation setting, keep-on-reload option, and whether the one-time notice was dismissed) are saved in the browser's extension storage on your device. This is why the extension asks for the "storage" permission. Settings do not contain any names or phone numbers.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send names, phone numbers, group names, settings or any other data to the developer or to any server.
- It makes no network requests of its own.
- It uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies or fingerprinting.
- It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
- It does not run code from remote servers. All code is included in the extension package.
- It does not use your data for credit, lending or any purpose other than exporting the list you asked for.

## Files you export

When you press **Finish & DL**, the extension creates a file (CSV, vCard or JSON) and saves it to your device through the browser's normal download. The extension does not keep a copy of it. Once the file is on your device, it is your responsibility to store, share and delete it.

## Your choices and control

- You decide when to start collecting (Start) and when to stop (closing the members list, or Finish & DL).
- You can clear the collected list at any time by pressing **Finish & DL**, or by closing the tab.
- You can remove all the extension's stored settings by removing the extension from your browser, or by clearing the extension's data in the browser settings.
- You can turn off the session copy of the list, and the country and unsaved-contacts filters, in the settings.

## Children

The extension is not intended for children under 16, the minimum age for WhatsApp in most countries.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension starts handling data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the "Last updated" date will change.

## Legal use

The extension processes personal data (names and phone numbers of group members) only on your device, for the purpose you choose. Using exported data is your responsibility under applicable data protection laws (for example GDPR, KVKK or other local laws) and WhatsApp's terms of service. Do not use the data to send unsolicited messages or for any purpose that the members did not agree to.
